Daily Chest Kitty — for Macs with Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4), macOS 12 or newer
==================================================================================

1. Drag "Daily Chest Kitty" into your Applications folder.

2. This little app isn't signed by Apple (that needs a paid developer
   account), so macOS needs one go-ahead the first time. Open Terminal
   (Applications → Utilities → Terminal), paste this line and press Return:

   xattr -cr "/Applications/Daily Chest Kitty.app" && codesign --force --deep --sign - "/Applications/Daily Chest Kitty.app"

   Nothing will be printed. That's normal.

3. Open "Daily Chest Kitty" from Applications.
   A paw appears in the menu bar at the top of the screen. The first
   time, the kitty will ask for a name.

Using it
--------
• Click the paw in the menu bar for today's chest and quests. Ticks are
  saved straight away and show up on the website too.
• Every so often the kitty walks across the screen, leaving paw prints.
  The prints never block clicks. The more quests get ticked, the braver
  the kitty becomes: first only prints, then a shadow, then a peek, and
  one day the whole kitty comes to sit at the bottom of the screen.
• The switches at the bottom of the panel pause the paw prints and turn
  "start when the Mac starts" on or off.
• Right-click the paw for a small menu, including Quit.

If macOS still refuses to open it: System Settings → Privacy & Security,
scroll down, and click "Open Anyway" next to Daily Chest Kitty.
